The shop

Cabinetmakers first, contractors second.

National Custom Millwork is a 10,000-square-foot fabrication shop in Beltsville, Maryland, equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and staffed by cabinetmakers who have been doing custom work for decades.

When you work with NCM, you work directly with the people who build — not a broker. That's why most of our work comes from repeat clients and word of mouth.
