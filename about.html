About NCM

The shop behind the work.

A fabrication shop of experienced cabinetmakers, building high-end architectural millwork in Beltsville, Maryland since 2006.

Our story

Two decades of custom work, one standard.

National Custom Millwork opened its doors in 2006 with a simple idea: clients deserve to work directly with the people who build their millwork. No brokers, no subcontracting the craft away — just a shop full of cabinetmakers who take pride in what leaves the floor.

Today NCM operates a 10,000-square-foot facility in Beltsville, Maryland, equipped with state-of-the-art machinery. We specialize in high-end architectural millwork and residential products: custom kitchens, vanities, fitted units, wood and laminate casework, wall panels, reception desks, entry doors, solid surface countertops, and conference tables.

Fabrication is only half the job. Our own professional installers carry every project through to the final walkthrough, so the hands that build your millwork are accountable for how it lands in the room.
